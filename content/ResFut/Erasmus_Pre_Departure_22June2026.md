### 3. Post-study work visas — Belgium, Portugal, UK

> [!warning] These rules change frequently — treat everything below as a starting point, not a final answer. Confirm directly with the relevant embassy/university international office before relying on it.

#### 🇧🇪 Belgium — clearest of the three
**The "Search Year" (Zoekjaar / Année de recherche)** — a 12-month, non-renewable residence permit to look for work or start a business, with full labour-market access.
- **Explicitly covers Erasmus Mundus mobility students**, not just Belgian-degree graduates — confirmed by Study in Flanders: *"You can also use a foreign degree... if you have followed a mobility programme in Belgium (such as Erasmus Mundus)."*
- If your degree is foreign (e.g. awarded by LUCA's consortium partner) but you did a mobility stint in Belgium, you apply at the **Belgian embassy/consulate within 3 months of receiving your diploma** — not in-country.
- Funds requirement: ~€800–900/month proof of means; health insurance required.
- 🔗 [Study in Flanders — orientation year](https://www.studyinflanders.be/after-your-studies/work-after-your-studies)
- 🔗 [Belgian Immigration Office (IBZ) — official rules](https://dofi.ibz.be/en/themes/third-country-nationals/study/orientation-year-after-completion-studies)
- 🔗 [CTRL-F — plain-language search year guide](https://ctrl-f.be/en/blog/candidates/the-search-year-for-non-eu-students-everything-you-need-to-know/)

#### 🇵🇹 Portugal — workable, but recently tightened
- **Article 122 (letters o & p), Law 23/2007** — lets you convert directly, *from inside Portugal*, from a study residence permit to a work residence permit once you have a job offer, without a new consulate visa. This is the main practical route and applies once you've held a Portuguese student residence permit (which you will, via your Aveiro semester).
- The old general **Job Seeker Visa** (4 months, open to almost anyone) was **abolished in October 2025** (Law 61/2025) and replaced with a stricter **Skilled Job Seeker Visa** aimed at professionals with 5+ years' experience — not really a fresh-graduate route.
- A separate **Post-Study Job Seeker Residence Permit** (12 months) exists but is restricted to **graduates of Portuguese institutions** specifically — worth clarifying whether a EMJM degree where Aveiro is one of three partners counts.
- 🔗 [LVP Advogados — Article 122 explainer](https://www.lvpadvogados.com/finished-your-studies-in-portugal-article-122-may-allow-you-to-stay-and-work)
- 🔗 [Facultet — Job Seeker Visa vs Student Visa, post Oct-2025 changes](https://www.facultet.school/blog/portugal-job-seeker-visa-vs-student-visa)
- 🔗 [Portuguese Embassy New Delhi — Erasmus Mundus E6 visa notice](https://novadeli.embaixadaportugal.mne.gov.pt/en/the-embassy/news/visa-applications-students-accepted-in-erasmus-mundus-joint-masters-at-portuguese-higher-education-institutions) *(confirms the E6 temporary-stay visa route via VFS Delhi for EMJM students bound for Portuguese institutions)*

#### 🇬🇧 UK — genuinely the gray area
The **Graduate Route** (post-study work visa) requires:
1. Holding a Student visa sponsored by the UK institution (Sheffield Hallam, "Track Record" sponsor) for the **"required minimum period"** of the course, and
2. The institution formally notifying the Home Office of course completion before you apply.

For courses **12 months or shorter**, Sheffield's own guidance is blunt: you must have studied the *full duration* inside the UK. For longer courses there's a "relevant period" rule that isn't spelled out in general terms — Glasgow's international office notes explicitly that **Erasmus Mundus students who study at partner institutions outside the UK "may still be eligible" if they held a Student visa for the full duration of the course**, which implies the visa (not necessarily physical UK presence) needs to span the whole programme — but this is determined case-by-case by the sponsoring university, not a fixed public rule.

Two more wrinkles:
- **Timing change**: from 1 January 2027, the Graduate visa shortens from 2 years to 18 months for Bachelor's/Master's graduates (PhDs unaffected). Depending on when Response_Able Futures' UK segment falls, this could matter.
- **High Potential Individual (HPI) visa** is a separate, sponsor-free route for graduates of ~40 top global universities *outside* the UK — not directly relevant unless one of the consortium partners (Aveiro, LUCA) is on that list, worth a quick check.

🔗 [Sheffield Hallam — After your course](https://www.shu.ac.uk/Study-here/International/After-your-course)
🔗 [University of Glasgow — Graduate visa, including Erasmus Mundus note](https://www.gla.ac.uk/international/support/visasandimmigration/graduatevisainformation/)
🔗 [University of Sheffield — "relevant period" of UK study explained](https://sheffield.ac.uk/student-visas-immigration/after-studies/working/graduate-route)
🔗 [UKCISA — Graduate route changes, 14 Oct 2025 statement](https://www.ukcisa.org.uk/news/student-update-changes-to-the-student-and-graduate-rules/)

### 4. Networking & ways to contribute — for Indians specifically

- **EU Alumni Directory** — Indian Erasmus+ beneficiaries can register on an EU-run directory that flags you for invitations to Delegation events (e.g. annual **"Erasmus Day"**) and helps with networking/job visibility. Ask the EU Delegation desk today how to register.
  🔗 [EEAS — 35th anniversary Erasmus+ event, mentions the directory](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/india/celebrating-35-years-eu-erasmus-programme-residence-france-india_en)

- **Erasmus Mundus Association — India Chapter (EMA-I)**, est. Dec 2007. Runs events, conferences, and a "Learn and Serve" peer-teaching initiative; partners with the EU Delegation on Pre-Departure Orientations like this one.
  🔗 [EMA-I overview](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/erasmus-mundus-association-india-emai/4522338)

- **This is the volunteering structure that matches what you want to do** (content, outreach, organising):
  - **Country Representative (CR)** — represents EMA nationally; runs the annual Pre-Departure Orientation; manages outreach, social media, and the country's EMA email.
  - **Programme Representative (PR)** — same idea but scoped to your specific programme (Response_Able Futures); link between EMA and your cohort/coordinators.
  - **OCB Ambassador** (Outreach & Community Building) — explicitly does event planning, comms, and promotional material — closest fit to "making posts, making a booklet."
  🔗 [EMA — Volunteer roles](https://www.em-a.eu/volunteer)
  🔗 [EMA — Country Representatives directory](https://www.em-a.eu/country-representatives)
