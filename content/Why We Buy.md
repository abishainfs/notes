> **Note:** This entire paragraph is super important. Most often design is looking to serve multiple user (& mis-user) groups at the same time, and more often than not, they are at loggerheads. Its super easy to target one and forget about the other, but it will show up and in ways that will make you look like the n00b designer you are.
