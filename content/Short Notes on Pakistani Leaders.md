
While Ramachandra Guha does not explicitly map out Pakistani leaders using the same specific color spectrum applied to Indian civil movements, Pakistani political history uses distinct metaphorical "colors" and ideological leanings to categorize its leadership eras:

1. The "Khaki" (Military Bureaucracy & Authoritarianism)Leaders: Ayub Khan, Yahya Khan, Zia-ul-Haq, and Pervez Musharraf.Leaning: This represents the pervasive influence of the military establishment (often referred to as the "Deep State"). Their ideological focus has historically leaned toward centralized state control, strict nationalism, and a national security-first paradigm, often interrupting civilian democratic processes.
2. The "Red" (Left-Wing Populism & Islamic Socialism)Leaders: Zulfikar Ali Bhutto and the early Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP).Leaning: Symbolized explicitly by the red in the PPP flag, this movement represented socialism, pro-labor rights, and populist economic reforms under the slogan "Roti, Kapda, Makaan" (Food, Clothing, Shelter). Over the decades, under Benazir Bhutto and later leadership, this shifted toward a center-left, progressive, and liberal democratic framework.
3. The "Deep Green" (Religious Conservatism & Islamization)Leaders: Muhammad Zia-ul-Haq (and various religious parties like Jamaat-e-Islami).Leaning: While green is the foundational color of Pakistan's flag representing Islamic heritage, a "deep green" ideological shift occurred primarily under Zia-ul-Haq's regime. His leaning was defined by state-sponsored Islamization, social conservatism, and aligning legal frameworks with religious orthodoxy to legitimize military rule.
4. The "Green & Gold" (Center-Right Conservatism & Free Market)Leaders: Nawaz Sharif and the Pakistan Muslim League-Nawaz (PML-N).Leaning: This represents traditional center-right politics, economic liberalism, and business-friendly policies. Backed heavily by the industrial and merchant classes of Punjab, their core focus historically centers on infrastructure development, privatization, and traditional political patronage.
5. The "Red & Green" (Populist Nationalism & Anti-Corruption Reform)Leaders: Imran Khan and Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI).Leaning: Reflected directly in the party's red and green flag, this leaning blends revolutionary populism with nationalist pride. Ideologically, it positions itself against the traditional dynastic political elite through an anti-corruption stance, youth mobilization, and the concept of Riyasat-e-Madina (an Islamic welfare state model).
