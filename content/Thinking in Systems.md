### Part One: System Structure and Behavior

> [!hl-yellow] [p. 25](https://play.google.com/books/reader?id=bOClMgAAAEAJ&pg=GBS.PA25.w.0.0.0.4) · 16 Aug 2023
> Is there anything that is not a system? Yes—a conglomeration without any particular interconnections or function.
>
> **Note:** Connections that have yet not been made, such as students on the first day of school, waiting to get to know each other. Leave elements by themselves long enough and they learn to interact with each other.

^p25-0004

>
> **Note:** https://fs.blog/iatrogenics/

^p26-0002

> [!hl-yellow] [p. 28](https://play.google.com/books/reader?id=bOClMgAAAEAJ&pg=GBS.PA28.w.0.0.0.2) · 17 Aug 2023

^p28-0002

> [!hl-yellow] [p. 29](https://play.google.com/books/reader?id=bOClMgAAAEAJ&pg=GBS.PA29.w.0.0.0.1) · 17 Aug 2023
> Purposes are deduced from behavior, not from rhetoric or stated goals.
>
> **Note:** Taking any system at it's face value is a cardinal sin. Observe it's behavior. Amber Krishan mentioned how we design for behaviours, not for problems or needs. There's a certain set of conditions under which a behaviour can occur, and designers create products or services that capitalize on this opportunity.

^p29-0001

> [!hl-yellow] [p. 30](https://play.google.com/books/reader?id=bOClMgAAAEAJ&pg=GBS.PA30.w.0.0.0.3) · 17 Aug 2023
> Changing elements usually has the least effect on the system.
>
> **Note:** Puts an end to the ship of Theseus problem once and for all. It's the purpose, and interconnections of a system that define it, rather than the elements that make it up. This definition is not very evident, but in most situations rings true.

^p30-0003

> [!hl-yellow] [p. 37](https://play.google.com/books/reader?id=bOClMgAAAEAJ&pg=GBS.PA37.w.0.0.0.4) · 17 Aug 2023
> Stocks generally change slowly, even when the flows into or out of them change suddenly. Therefore, stocks act as delays or buffers or shock absorbers in systems.
>
> **Note:** A perfect business idea capitalizes on the fact that there is "X" stock, and it's flowing in or out of a system at "Y" rate, and people are neither expecting or prepared for the movement. Even better ideas are at the end of multiple stock flows. Eg. Sustainability laws being passed > increase in demand for renewables > solar panels increase in price > borosil makes glass for solar panels regardless of manufacturer > invest in borosil
