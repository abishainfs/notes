So the Drift chair started long ago as a project that [[Kenneth Rodrigues]] called the capsule chair and was going to be a capsule, a bent in a certain angle like capsule extruded, and then at some point bent to form this building block that we use throughout the chair. This is something that he worked on at university at CSM, which was one of his graduation projects where, he was inspired by Barber Osgerby and the Bouroullec brothers and their use of wood and metal cast metal, using the liquid properties of cast metal and the strength and beauty and the the sort of resolute hardness of wood. And then they come together in, in a very stunning way, in many different products. For example, the steel wood chair by the Bouroullec brothers, a good example.

So the capsule chair was born out of a desire to express wood in that way. And instead of the the part where the cast metal steps in to alleviate the hardness of wood, the intent was to do a bent wood. Expression which helps give that dynamic ness, which we can then use to make different parts out of it.
