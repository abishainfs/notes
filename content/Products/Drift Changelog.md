## **Finn Juhl - The key idea: “carried” vs “carrying”**

Almost every important Finn Juhl chair after 1945 revolves around one breakthrough:

- The **frame carries**.
- The **seat and back are carried**.

Prior Danish furniture typically integrated structure and seating into one system. Juhl separated them visually and structurally, creating the famous “floating seat” effect seen in the 45 Chair and Chieftain. This sounds simple, but it fundamentally changed how the chair was read.

Compare:
- Wegner = structure is the chair.
- Juhl = structure and body are independent elements. see https://www.thechieftainchair.com/thedetails
