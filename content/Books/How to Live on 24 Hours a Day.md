#books 

Author: Arnold Bennett

### Intro
The book is a short treatise, that urges us to rethink the way we approach _the use of our time_, to thrive during our regular workweeks.

### Takeaways

- The most important takeaway was "don't be a snob when you start practicing this." We aren't better or worse than other people because they do (or don't ) things that we think are valuable.
